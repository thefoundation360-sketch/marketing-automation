# Service Offers + Pricing Structure
## Built to Close in 6 Days

---

## The Core Rule: One Offer Per Call

Don't present a menu. Present one thing, priced to remove risk, structured to get cash in hand today.

**Deposit-first model**: Every offer requires 50% upfront before you touch anything. Frame it as "I start building the moment deposit clears — most clients see it live within 48–72 hours."

---

## Offer 1: The "Never Miss a Lead" Package
**Target Niche**: Home Service Companies (HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing, Pest Control)

### What They Get
1. **Missed Call Text-Back** — When a call goes unanswered, an auto-SMS fires within 60 seconds: *"Hey, this is [Business]. Sorry we missed you — reply here or call us back at [number]. We'll get you taken care of today."*
2. **Quote Follow-Up Sequence** — After you send a quote, 3 automated touchpoints go out over 5 days: Day 1 SMS ("Just checking you got the quote"), Day 3 email ("Any questions?"), Day 5 SMS ("We have an opening this week if you want to move forward").
3. **Appointment Reminder System** — SMS 24 hours before + 2 hours before scheduled appointment, with 1-tap confirm/reschedule link.

### Tools to Build It
- **Zapier** (if they use Jobber/Housecall Pro) — most triggers are pre-built
- **GoHighLevel** (full-stack, easiest to white-label)
- **Twilio + Google Sheets** (lowest cost DIY for basic missed call + SMS)
- You can build the core of this in 3–5 hours using GHL or Zapier

### Pricing
| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Starter | **$800** | Missed call text-back + 1 follow-up sequence |
| Core *(recommend this)* | **$1,200** | All 3 automations above |
| Full Stack | **$1,800** | Core + Google review request automation + monthly reporting |

**Deposit**: 50% upfront ($400 / $600 / $900)
**Monthly Retainer** (optional upsell after delivery): $197–$297/month to maintain, update sequences, and monitor

### Delivery Timeline
- Starter: 24–48 hours
- Core: 48–72 hours
- Full Stack: 3–5 days

### ROI Script for the Call
*"If your average job is $350 and you're missing 2 calls a day, that's $700/day in potential revenue walking out the door. My system catches those leads automatically. You only need 3 recovered jobs to make back your investment — that's usually within the first week."*

## Offer 2: The "Never Lose a Booking" Package
**Target Niche**: Med Spas, Aesthetic Clinics, Beauty Salons

### What They Get
1. **Instagram/Facebook DM Auto-Responder** — Instant reply to DMs with a booking link and FAQ answers, 24/7. Built on ManyChat (free–$15/month for the client).
2. **No-Show Reduction System** — SMS reminder 48 hours before + day-of confirmation. If no confirm, auto-text "Are you still coming?" with option to reschedule.
3. **Reactivation Campaign** — One-time blast to past clients who haven't booked in 90+ days: *"We miss you — here's 10% off your next [treatment]. Book this week only."*

### Tools to Build It
- **ManyChat** — Instagram/FB DM automation (2-hour setup)
- **Acuity Scheduling or Square Appointments** — usually already have this
- **Twilio or SimpleTexting** — SMS reminders
- **Google Sheets / Airtable** — client reactivation list management

### Pricing
| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| DM Responder Only | **$600** | Instagram/FB DM auto-response |
| Core *(recommend this)* | **$1,000** | DM responder + no-show SMS system |
| Growth | **$1,500** | Core + reactivation campaign + monthly nurture sequence |

**Deposit**: 50% upfront ($300 / $500 / $750)
**Monthly Retainer**: $197–$397/month (ManyChat management, sequence updates, reactivation blasts)

### Delivery Timeline
- DM Responder: 24 hours
- Core: 48–72 hours
- Growth: 4–5 days

### ROI Script for the Call
*"You told me you get 3 no-shows a week at $400 per slot — that's $1,200 a week in lost revenue. My system will cut that by 60–80% based on what I've seen with other clinics. Even if it only prevents 1 no-show per week, it pays for itself in under a month. Your investment is $1,000 one time."*

## Offer 3: The "Dead Leads Revival" Package
**Target Niche**: Real Estate Agents

### What They Get
1. **New Lead Speed-to-Contact** — Instant SMS + email fires the second a new Zillow/Realtor.com lead comes in. No more manual follow-up lag.
2. **Long-Term Nurture Sequence** — 90-day automated email + SMS drip for leads who aren't ready to buy yet. 12-touch sequence with market updates, listings, and check-ins.
3. **Database Reactivation** — One campaign to every cold contact in their CRM with a low-friction "Are you still looking?" text.

### Tools to Build It

| Core *(recommend this)* | **$1,100** | Speed-to-contact + 90-day nurture |

**Deposit**: 50% upfront
**Monthly Retainer**: $150–$300/month

## The Upsell Conversation (run this after delivery, not before)

Once the automation is live and they've seen results (day 3–7), say this:

*"Now that the foundation is running, most of my clients add the monthly maintenance plan. For $[197-297]/month I monitor everything, update your sequences as needed, and add one new automation per quarter. It's cheaper than one missed job. Want me to set that up?"*

This turns a one-time $1,000 deal into $3,400 in year-one revenue.
