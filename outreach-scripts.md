# Outreach Scripts + Call Guide
## Cold Call, DM, and Follow-Up Scripts

---

## SCRIPT 1: Home Service Companies (HVAC / Plumbing / Roofing)

### Cold Call Opening (First 15 Seconds)

> "Hey, is this [Owner Name]? — Great. My name is [Your Name], I work with home service companies to recover revenue from missed calls and ghosted quotes. I'm not trying to waste your time — quick question: roughly how many calls a week do you think go to voicemail without getting called back?"

**Wait for their answer. They'll usually say 5–20. Then:**

> "Yeah, that tracks. Most of the HVAC and plumbing guys I talk to are leaving $2,000–$5,000 on the table every week just from that alone. I built a system that texts those leads back within 60 seconds, automatically. You got 3 minutes?"

---

### Discovery Questions (Pick 2–3, Don't Run Through All)

1. "What does a typical job run you — average ticket?"
2. "When a lead calls and you can't answer, what happens to that lead right now?"
3. "After you send a quote, how do you follow up — or does it just sit there?"
4. "Do you use any software right now — Jobber, Housecall Pro, anything like that?"
5. "What's your busiest time of year, and are you booked up or still filling your calendar?"

**Listen for**: missed calls, quotes that go cold, no-show appointments, high ad spend with low close rate. These are your hooks.

---

### Transition to Offer

> "Okay so here's what I do. I set up three automations for you: one that texts missed calls back within a minute, one that follows up on quotes automatically over 5 days so you don't have to chase people, and one that reminds your booked jobs so you cut no-shows. Takes me 48 hours to build. Most clients recover the investment in the first week or two from jobs they would have otherwise lost. Investment is $1,200 total — $600 to start and $600 when I hand it over."

---

### Objection Handling

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Totally fair — what's the main thing you'd want to think through? Is it budget, or more like you want to make sure it'll actually work for your setup?"

*(They'll tell you the real objection. Address that specific thing.)*

> "Here's what I'd suggest — let me send you one example of exactly what the missed-call text looks like, takes 30 seconds to read. If it makes sense, great. If not, no pressure. Can I text it to you right now?"

**"What's the ROI?"**
> "Depends on your average job size. You said around $400. If the system recovers 3 jobs a month you would have otherwise lost — that's $1,200/month. My system costs $1,200 once. You're in the green after month one and it keeps running forever. That's a 12:1 return in year one."

**"We already have someone / I already have a system"**
> "That's great — what are you using? [Listen]. Got it. A lot of the guys I work with are paying for software that has the capability but they haven't set it up yet, or it's not actually sending the texts. When did you last check if the automation is firing correctly?"

*(This plants doubt without being pushy. Most of the time they don't actually have it running.)*

**"I can't afford it right now"**
> "Understood — cash flow timing is real. Here's a question: if the system recovered one extra job this week, what would that be worth to you? [They say $400–$800]. So you'd have that money before your balance is even due. I only take $600 upfront — if that's too much right now we can talk about timing, but I want to make sure this isn't just getting pushed because of cashflow when it'd pay for itself immediately."

---

### The Close

> "Here's what I'd suggest — let me get started for you. I'll take the $600 deposit today, have the first automation live within 24 hours so you can see it working, and I'll collect the balance when everything's set up. I send an invoice through Stripe — takes 2 minutes. Want me to send that over now?"

**Silence after the close. Don't fill it.**

---

### Follow-Up DM (If They Don't Respond to Cold Outreach)

Send this via text or Instagram DM, 24 hours after first contact:

> "Hey [Name] — [Your Name] here, tried to reach you earlier. Completely understand if the timing was off. I work specifically with [HVAC/plumbing] companies to automate their missed call follow-up and quote reminders. If you're losing jobs to voicemail and cold quotes, I built something that fixes that in 48 hours. Worth a 10-minute call this week? I'll show you exactly what it looks like before you commit to anything."

## SCRIPT 2: Med Spas & Aesthetic Clinics

### Cold DM (Instagram or Facebook — Most Effective Channel for This Niche)

Find them by searching Instagram for "[city] med spa", "[city] botox", "[city] aesthetic clinic". DM the business account:

> "Hey [Name] — I help med spas and aesthetic clinics in [city] stop losing clients to slow DM responses and no-shows. I noticed your page has a lot of engagement — are you using any automation to respond to inquiries after hours, or is that all manual right now?"

**If they reply, pivot to:**

> "Totally — most of the clinics I work with are handling DMs themselves and it's eating hours every week. I set up an Instagram auto-responder that answers booking questions instantly, 24/7, and a no-show reduction system that cuts cancellations by 60–80%. Would it be worth 15 minutes to see if it'd work for your setup?"

---

### Cold Call Opening (If You Have Their Number)

> "Hi, is [Owner Name] available? — I'm [Your Name], I work with aesthetic clinics on automating their booking process so they stop losing clients to slow responses and no-shows. Quick question — when a new client DMs you on Instagram after hours, what happens to that message?"

**Wait. Then:**

> "Yeah. Most of the clinic owners I talk to check it in the morning and by then the client already booked somewhere else. I built a system that responds instantly, any time of day, and books them into your calendar automatically. You got 5 minutes?"

---

### Discovery Questions

1. "How many DMs or inquiries do you get in a typical week?"
2. "What percentage of those actually book — do you track that?"
3. "How many no-shows are you dealing with per week, and what's your average treatment price?"
4. "Do you have a system right now for reaching out to clients who haven't been back in a few months?"
5. "Are you on ManyChat or any automation tool already?"

---

### Transition to Offer

> "Okay, based on what you're telling me — [X] no-shows a week at [Y] per slot, and most of your inquiries are coming through Instagram. I can fix both of those for you. The DM responder goes live in 24 hours. The no-show system takes another day or two. Total investment is $1,000. You put $500 down now, and $500 when I hand it over. You only need to prevent 2–3 no-shows to get your money back."

---

### Objection Handling

**"Send me more info first"**
> "Sure — what specifically would be most helpful? I don't want to send you a PDF you won't have time to read. Can I just text you a 60-second video of exactly what your clients would see when they DM you? That'll answer it better than anything I could write."

**"We already have Acuity / Jane App"**
> "Perfect — I actually work with those. The booking system is separate from the DM automation. Acuity handles the booking once someone clicks the link — I handle getting them to click the link in the first place. Those two things work together, not against each other."

**"I need to run it by my business partner"**
> "Of course — when are you two talking next? I'll stay out of your way. One thing that might help: if I send a quick breakdown of the ROI math so you both have the same numbers, that usually makes the conversation faster. What's a good email to send that to?"

---

### The Close

> "I'll tell you what — let me build the DM responder for free as a proof of concept. Takes me about an hour. If you see it working and it feels right, you pay me $1,000 and I build out the full system. If you hate it, you owe me nothing. Fair?"

*(This removes all risk and almost always results in a paid engagement — because once they see it working, they're sold.)*

---

### Follow-Up Sequence (If No Response to DM)

**Day 2:**
> "Hey [Name] — just following up on my message. Not trying to be annoying — if the timing is off just say so and I'll leave you alone. But if you're still manually responding to DMs and dealing with no-shows, I have something that solves both this week. Worth a quick call?"

**Day 5 (Final):**
> "[Name] — last message, I promise. I'm going to stop reaching out after this. I work with 2–3 clinics per month and I have one spot left this month. If automating your bookings is something you've been thinking about, now's the time. If not, no hard feelings — good luck with the business."

*(Scarcity + finality creates action.)*

## Universal Rules for Every Outreach

1. **Lead with a question, not a pitch.** A question gets a response; a pitch gets ignored.
2. **Mirror their language.** If they say "clients" use "clients." If they say "patients" use "patients."
3. **Never leave voicemail on the first call.** Call twice in 30 minutes. If no answer, text: "Hey [Name], tried calling — [Your Name] here. Got something that might help with [specific pain]. Text me back when you have a sec."
4. **Always ask for the next concrete step.** "Can I send you the video?" "Can we do a call Thursday at 2?" Never leave a call without a commitment.
5. **Follow up 5 times before you give up.** Most closes happen on follow-up #3 or #4.
