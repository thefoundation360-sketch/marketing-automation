# Cold Call Script — "I already built it for you" close

The whole idea: don't sell a website, **book a look at one you already made**. Free, no pressure. You're just asking which day.

---

## The opener (say it warm, a little casual)

> "Hey, how's it going? — real quick, my name's **[Name]**, I build websites for local **[barbershops / food trucks / salons]**.
> I came across your **[Instagram / Facebook]** — looks like y'all are busy, business is doing good — and I noticed you don't really have a website yet.
> So I actually went ahead and **built a sample one for you**. No charge, no obligation.
> I'd just love to show it to you — you around **Tuesday or Wednesday** to take a quick look?"

**Why each line is there:**
- *"How's it going"* — human, not a robocall.
- *"I build websites for [their exact type]"* — instant relevance.
- *"I saw your Instagram, you're busy"* — proof you did homework; flatters them.
- *"I already built a sample for you"* — the hook. Curiosity + they feel special.
- *"No charge, no obligation"* — kills the wall before it goes up.
- *"Tuesday or Wednesday?"* — assumptive close. Two yeses, not a yes/no.

---

## Objection comebacks

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "The design's free to look at — costs you nothing. If you love it, it's [$X one-time or $Y/month] and I handle everything. But honestly, just see if you even like it first — no pressure either way."

**"I don't have time / I'm working."**
> "Totally get it, that's exactly why I already did the work for you. It's a 5-minute look — I can text you the link right now, or catch you [tomorrow morning]. What's better?"

**"I already have a website / a guy."**
> "Nice — is it bringing you new customers, or just kinda sitting there? Take a look at mine free, and if yours is already better, no harm done."

**"Just text me the info."**
> "For sure — what's the best number to text the link to? And cool if I check back with you [Thursday] to see what you thought?"

**"Not interested."**
> "No worries at all — mind if I text you the sample anyway? If it's not for you, just ignore it. What's your best number?"

---

## Voicemail (keep it under 15 seconds)

> "Hey [name], it's [Name] — I build websites for [their type] and I actually put a free sample together for your shop. No cost, just wanted you to see it. Give me a ring back at [your number], or I'll try you again tomorrow. Thanks!"

---

## IMPORTANT — make it true

The pitch says *"I already built one for you."* To close hard and stay honest, **have at least a quick demo ready before you dial** — even a one-page mockup with their name, logo, and a couple photos pulled from their Instagram. When they say "sure, show me," you send a link on the spot and the deal basically closes itself.

Tip: batch-build 5–10 demos at a time (same template, swap the name/photos/colors) so you always have one ready for whoever picks up.

---

## Flow

1. Work the leads top-down (Tier 1 first — best chance to answer + need it).
2. Answered → run the opener → book the look or text the demo link.
3. Log the outcome in `contacted-ledger.csv` (called / voicemail / interested / sold / dead).
4. Next batch is generated excluding everyone already in the ledger — no repeats.
