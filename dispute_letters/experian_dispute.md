## LEGAL GROUNDS FOR DISPUTE

1. **FCRA § 611(a)** — Right to dispute and demand reinvestigation
2. **FCRA § 605(a)(1)** — Cases under bankruptcy must be removed after 10 years
3. **FCRA § 605(a)(4)** — All other adverse items (charge-offs, collections, late payments) must be removed after 7 years from DOFD
4. **FCRA § 605(a)(3)(B)** — Inquiries older than 2 years must be removed
5. **FCRA § 623(a)(1)** — Furnishers must report only accurate information
6. **FCRA § 623(a)(5)** — Furnishers must report the correct date of first delinquency
7. **FCRA § 623(b)** — Furnishers must investigate and correct disputes forwarded by CRAs
8. **FCRA § 615(a)** — Any creditor who takes adverse action based on my report must notify me
