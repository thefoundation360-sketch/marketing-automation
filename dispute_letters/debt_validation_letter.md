## NOTICE OF DEBT VALIDATION REQUEST

To Whom It May Concern:

This letter is sent pursuant to my rights under the **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. § 1692 et seq.**, specifically **Section 809(b) (15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b))**.

I am writing in response to your contact on [DATE] regarding the alleged debt referenced above. **This is not a refusal to pay, but a formal request for verification and validation of this alleged debt before any further action is taken.**

---

## WHAT I AM DEMANDING YOU PROVIDE

Per FDCPA § 809(b), I request that you cease all collection activity, including any credit bureau reporting, until you provide me with **complete validation** of this debt, including:

1. **Complete account history** showing how the alleged balance of $[AMOUNT] was calculated, including the original principal, interest charges, fees, and any other additions
2. **A copy of the original signed contract** or agreement that created this alleged debt with the original creditor
3. **Proof that your company is licensed** to collect debts in the state of [YOUR STATE] (license number and state of licensure)
4. **Proof that your company is legally authorized** to collect this specific debt — chain of title (assignment agreements showing the debt was transferred from original creditor to any intermediate owners to your agency)
5. **The name and address of the original creditor**
6. **The date of first delinquency** with the original creditor
7. **A copy of the last billing statement** from the original creditor
8. **Any and all agreements** between your agency and the original creditor authorizing you to collect this debt

---

## CEASE COLLECTION ACTIVITY NOTICE

Until you provide the above validation:

- **DO NOT** contact me by phone, mail, email, or any other means other than to provide the requested validation
- **DO NOT** report, update, or continue reporting this account to any credit reporting agency (TransUnion, Experian, or Equifax)
- **DO NOT** sell, transfer, or assign this alleged debt to another collector

Any continued collection activity before validation is provided is a **violation of FDCPA § 809(b)** and subjects your agency to civil liability under **FDCPA § 813**, including:
- Actual damages
- Statutory damages up to **$1,000 per violation**
- Attorney fees and court costs
