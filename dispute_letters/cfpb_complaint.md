## TIPS FOR FILING YOUR CFPB COMPLAINT

1. **File one complaint per company** (you'll need to file separately for TransUnion, Experian, and Equifax)
2. **Attach your dispute letter and any responses** you received as supporting documents
3. **Track your complaint number** — CFPB will email you a confirmation
4. **The company has 15 calendar days** to respond to you through the CFPB portal, and may be given an additional 60 days in complex cases
5. **Your complaint becomes public** (anonymized) in the CFPB Consumer Complaint Database — this creates accountability
6. **Follow up** if you don't hear back within 30 days

> **IMPORTANT:** Keep copies of every letter you send and every response you receive. Always send dispute letters via **Certified Mail, Return Receipt Requested** and keep the green card as proof of delivery. The 30-day clock starts when TransUnion/Experian/Equifax **receives** your letter.
