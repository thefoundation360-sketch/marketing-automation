## LEGAL GROUNDS FOR DISPUTE

1. **FCRA § 611(a)** — Consumer's right to dispute inaccurate information; CRA duty to reinvestigate
2. **FCRA § 605(a)(4)** — Accounts delinquent more than 7 years from DOFD must be excluded
3. **FCRA § 605(c)** — Running of the 7-year period from date of commencement of delinquency
4. **FCRA § 623(a)(1)** — Furnisher prohibition on reporting known inaccurate information
5. **FCRA § 623(a)(2)** — Furnisher duty to correct and update inaccurate information
6. **FCRA § 623(a)(5)** — Furnisher must report accurate date of first delinquency
7. **FCRA § 623(b)** — Furnisher must investigate disputes forwarded by CRAs
8. **FCRA § 623(a)(8)** — Debt collector / furnisher dispute obligations
9. **FTC "Furnisher Rule" (16 C.F.R. Part 660)** — Regulatory requirements for furnisher accuracy

## RE-AGING NOTICE

I specifically put Equifax on notice that I believe one or more accounts have been **impermissibly re-aged** — that is, the Date of First Delinquency has been manipulated to make the account appear newer than it is, thereby extending the 7-year reporting window in violation of **FCRA § 623(a)(5)** and **FTC v. TRW, Inc.** precedent. Any such re-aged account must be deleted immediately.
