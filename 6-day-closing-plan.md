# 6-Day Closing Plan
## From Zero to $800–$2,000 — Exact Daily Actions

---

## The Math First

To hit $800: close 1 deal at $1,200 (collect $600 deposit Day 1–2, $600 on delivery)
To hit $2,000: close 2 deals at $1,000–$1,200 each

You need to contact 20–30 businesses to close 2 deals. That's realistic in 6 days if you work 3–5 focused hours per day on outreach.

**Revenue model**: Deposit collected = real money. Start delivery immediately to collect the balance fast.

---

## DAY 1 — Build Your List & Make First Contacts

### Morning (2 hours): Build a 30-Prospect List

**For Home Service Companies:**
1. Go to Google Maps: search "HVAC [your city]", "plumbing [your city]", "pest control [your city]"
2. Open every listing that has: a real phone number, 10+ Google reviews, and looks like a local owner-operated business (not a franchise)
3. Add to CRM: Business Name, Phone, Owner Name (from website/Google), any reviews that mention slow response or scheduling issues

**For Med Spas:**
1. Go to Instagram: search "[city] med spa", "[city] botox", "[city] aesthetic"
2. Look for pages with 500–5,000 followers, active posting, DMs enabled
3. Add to CRM: Business Name, Instagram handle, website, estimated size from post engagement

**Target**: 15 home service + 10 med spa = 25 prospects total

### Afternoon (2–3 hours): First Contact Blitz

**Phone calls first** (for home service):
- Block 2 hours, call 15 businesses back-to-back
- Use the opening script exactly as written — don't improvise until you've done 10 calls
- Goal: book 3–5 conversations, not close deals (not yet)

**DMs second** (for med spa):
- Send the Instagram DM script to 10 med spas
- Personalize the first line with something from their page: "Saw your reel on [treatment] — love the content"
- Don't send the same message copy-paste to all 10 — vary the opening

**End of Day Target:**
- 15 cold calls made
- 10 DMs sent
- 2–4 callbacks or responses received
- 1 discovery call booked

---

## DAY 2 — Discovery Calls & First Close Attempt

### Morning: Work Your Callbacks

- Every business that called back or responded: run the full discovery call script
- Ask all your discovery questions
- Transition to the offer at the end of every call
- **Goal: present your offer to at least 2 people today**

**Handling "I need to think about it":**
Don't hang up without booking the follow-up call. "Makes sense — let's do this: I'll send you the breakdown of what I build and we can talk Thursday at 10. Does that work?"

### Afternoon: Second Wave of Outreach

- Add 10 more prospects to your list
- Call back anyone who didn't answer yesterday (call at a different time — try after 5pm for owner-operators)
- Send Day 2 follow-up DMs to anyone who didn't respond yesterday

**End of Day Target:**
- 2 discovery calls completed
- 1 offer presented
- 1 deposit ideally collected (if not today, set it up for Day 3)
- 25 total prospects in CRM

---

## DAY 3 — Close Your First Deal, Start Delivery

### Morning: Chase the Hot Leads

Call everyone who said "let me think about it" or "sounds interesting":

> "Hey [Name] — [Your Name]. We spoke yesterday about the missed call system. I wanted to follow up — I had a spot open up this week so I can start immediately if you want to move forward. Did you have any questions I can answer?"

**The goal today**: collect your first deposit. $500–$600 hits your account = momentum.

**When they say yes:**
- Send a Stripe invoice immediately (Stripe.com, free to set up, takes 2 minutes to create an invoice)
- While you're on the call: "I'll send the invoice right now — you should get it in about 30 seconds. Does [email] still work?"
- Stay on the line while they pay if possible. Reduces drop-off dramatically.

### Afternoon: Start Building

- Once deposit is received, start the automation build immediately
- For HVAC missed call text-back: set up GHL or Zapier, configure the SMS trigger, test it with their phone number
- For med spa DM responder: set up ManyChat, configure the Instagram auto-response flow, test with a test DM
- Delivery speed = trust = faster final payment

**End of Day Target:**
- First deposit collected ($400–$600)
- Build in progress
- 5 more prospect calls made to keep pipeline moving

---

## DAY 4 — Deliver Deal #1, Close Deal #2

### Morning: Deliver and Collect Balance

Complete the build for your first client. Test every automation. Then:

> "Hey [Name] — [Your Name]. Your system is live. I just tested it — sent a test call to your number and the text fired within 45 seconds. I want to walk you through it on a quick 10-minute call so you know exactly how it works. When can you do that today?"

Walk them through everything on a screen share or video. Then:

> "Everything looks good — I'll send the final invoice now for the remaining balance. You should see it in about 30 seconds."

**Collect balance: $400–$600.** You've now hit $800–$1,200.

### Afternoon: Close Deal #2

Your hottest lead from the pipeline should be ready for a close call by now. Run your discovery/offer sequence. Collect deposit.

**End of Day Target:**
- Deal #1 fully delivered and paid
- Deal #2 deposit collected
- At $1,200–$1,600 total

---

## DAY 5 — Deliver Deal #2, Upsell Retainer

### Morning: Build and Deliver Deal #2

Same process: build, test, walk through with client, send final invoice.

### Afternoon: Upsell Conversation

After delivering and collecting final payment:

> "[Name] — now that everything's running, I want to mention something. I have a small group of clients I maintain monthly — I monitor the automations, update sequences when they go stale, and add one new thing per quarter. For $[197–297]/month it's hands-off for you completely. Want me to add that?"

**Most clients who just saw results say yes.** This is recurring revenue.

### Also Today: Open Deal #3 Conversation

Keep calling. Even if you hit your target, deals #3 and #4 become next week's income or your retainer stack. The pipeline should never go cold.

**End of Day Target:**
- Deal #2 fully delivered and paid
- 1 retainer upsell pitched
- $1,800–$2,200 total collected

---

## DAY 6 — Cash Out & Build Pipeline for Week 2

### Morning: Collect Any Outstanding Balances

- Follow up on any invoices not paid
- Send a friendly reminder: "Hey [Name] — just a heads up the invoice is still open. Shoot me a message if anything came up on your end."

### Afternoon: Debrief and Scale

1. Review your CRM — who's still warm but hasn't closed?
2. Send final follow-up to anyone who ghosted
3. Ask your 2 closed clients for a referral: "By the way — do you know any other [HVAC owners / clinic owners] who deal with the same issues? I'm opening 2 spots next week."

*(Referral from a happy client closes faster than any cold call.)*

**End of Day Target:**
- $800–$2,000+ collected
- 2–3 prospects still warm for next week
- 1 referral request sent

## What to Do If You Stall (No Responses by Day 3)

1. **Change the channel.** If calls aren't working, try DMs. If DMs aren't working, try LinkedIn for the real estate niche.
2. **Change the opener.** If you've done 20 calls and no one will talk, your opening isn't landing. Go back to the script — are you asking a question or pitching immediately?
3. **Lower the entry price.** Offer the $600 Starter instead of the $1,200 Core. Get a client in the door. Upsell later.
4. **Go local in person.** Walk into 5 local HVAC or service company offices. Drop off a one-page summary with your number. Say "the owner is expecting my call" when you follow up. It works.
5. **Offer a free test.** "Let me build the DM responder for free and show you it working. If you like it, we talk about a retainer." Free tests almost always convert.
