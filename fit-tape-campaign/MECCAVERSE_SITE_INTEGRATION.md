Heal Hop is a movement — and a method.

It's hip hop built for people who use physical movement to process, 
heal, and grow. Not party music. Not background noise. 
Music that moves with you while you move through it.

The Heal Hop Method combines:
→ Physical movement (working out, dancing, running, any movement)

→ The healing that happens when you commit to showing up

This is what The Fit Tape was built to do.
