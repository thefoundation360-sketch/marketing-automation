## VERDICT: IS IT WORTH BOOSTING?

**YES — but NOT the way you have it set up right now.**

Your current setup ($3/day, 1 day) will get you ~13 estimated website visits.
Even at a 10% conversion rate (very optimistic), that's 1.3 clicks to your link tree.
That is **$3 burned with near-zero chance of a GumRoad sale.**

The algorithm needs a MINIMUM of 5–7 days to learn who responds to your ad.
Running for 1 day gives it no data. The money is wasted.

**The rule: Never boost for less than 5 days. Better to wait and do it right.**

## WHAT'S WRONG WITH YOUR CURRENT SETUP

### Problem 1 — Budget & Duration
```
CURRENT:  $3 / 1 day = 13 est. website visits
FIX:      $5–7 / 5–7 days = ~150–400 est. website visits
```
Meta's algorithm needs 50+ conversions in 7 days to optimize properly.
At $3/day you'll never hit that threshold.

### Problem 2 — Age Range Too Wide
```
CURRENT:  25 – 65 yr (ALL)
FIX:      25 – 42 yr
```
Research data: Fitness and wellness content on Meta converts strongest in the 25–38 age bracket.
The 45–65 bracket has high CPMs but low conversion for music/digital products.
Cutting this REDUCES your cost per click significantly.

### Problem 3 — Interests Are Hip-Hop-Only, Missing the Fitness Layer
```
CURRENT:  Underground hip hop, Kanye West, Southern hip hop, J. Cole, 
          Self-confidence, Kendrick Lamar, Chance The Rapper

PROBLEM:  These are music-fan interests. They'll find people who like rap —
          not people who work out, dance, and buy wellness content.
          
FIX:      Layer in fitness/dance interests (see below)
```

### Problem 4 — Location Is Regional When Budget Is Tiny
```
CURRENT:  Atlanta, Memphis, Charlotte, Hendersonville, Murfreesboro, 
          Nashville, Clarksville, Smyrna, Birmingham

ISSUE:    Too many cities spread a small budget thin.
          You're paying to reach each city's audience separately.
          
FIX (Option A): Go national — let Advantage+ find your people anywhere
FIX (Option B): Pick YOUR #1 city only — cheaper CPM, laser focused
```

## THE FUNNEL — REDUCE FRICTION TO INCREASE SALES

Every extra click loses ~40–50% of traffic. Right now your funnel is:

```
Reel → "Link in bio" → Linktree → (multiple options) → GumRoad
```

That's potentially 3 clicks before someone buys. You lose half your traffic at each step.

**Fix the funnel:**

```
OPTION A (Best for GumRoad sales):
Reel → Bio link → GumRoad bundle page directly
(Remove Linktree from this path — send the ad traffic straight to GumRoad)

OPTION B (Best for overall awareness):
Reel → Bio link → Meccaverse Fit Tape page (with GumRoad embed/button prominent)

OPTION C (Best hybrid — use a link page with MAX 3 options):
Reel → Bio link → Linktree with ONLY:
  1. Stream The Fit Tape (Disco.ac)
  2. Buy The Bundle (GumRoad)
  3. Join the community (email opt-in)
```

**Caption on the boosted Reel MUST include a direct CTA:**
- Bad: "Link in bio 🔗"
- Good: "Get The Fit Tape bundle — link in bio 🎧💪"
- Best: "The Fit Tape is OUT. Full EP + [describe bundle benefit] in bio. Tap it."

## WHAT TO WATCH IN THE FIRST 48 HOURS

Check these numbers in your Instagram Insights / Meta Ads Manager:

| Metric | What's Good | What's Bad |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| CPM (cost per 1,000 views) | Under $15 | Over $25 |
| CPC (cost per link click) | Under $1.50 | Over $3 |
| CTR (click-through rate) | Over 1.5% | Under 0.5% |
| Video plays to 50%+ | Over 30% | Under 15% |
| Profile visits from ad | Growing daily | Flat/zero |

**If after 3 days:** CTR is under 0.5%, PAUSE the ad. The creative isn't converting. Either:
- Switch to a different Reel (try a slower, more emotional track)
- Change the caption CTA
- Narrow to women-only audience

## THE REEL CHECKLIST (for the one you're boosting)

Before you boost any Reel, confirm it has ALL of these:

- [ ] Hook in first 1–2 seconds (movement, energy, or a bold statement)
- [ ] Your best audio playing immediately
- [ ] Text on screen visible without sound (captions or lyrics overlay)
- [ ] Clear message about WHO this is for
- [ ] Verbal or text CTA: "Link in bio" or "Stream now" or "Get the bundle"
- [ ] Under 30 seconds (30-sec Reels get boosted to more non-followers)
- [ ] No TikTok watermark (Meta suppresses these — download from Canva or native)
